Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Eagle Hubs uses cookies and similar technologies on eaglehubspk.com (the "Site").

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help websites remember information about your visit, such as preferences or usage patterns.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies set by Google Analytics (GA4) and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the Site — which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, and which links are clicked. This helps us improve the Site's content, layout, and performance. We do not use cookies for advertising retargeting.

Third-Party Cookies

When you click through to WhatsApp, Instagram, or our careers portal, those services may set their own cookies in accordance with their own policies. Eagle Hubs does not control third-party cookies.

Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings. You can also use browser extensions or Google's opt-out tools to limit Google Analytics data collection. Disabling cookies may affect how some parts of the Site behave, but the Site will remain browsable.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time; changes take effect when posted on this page.
